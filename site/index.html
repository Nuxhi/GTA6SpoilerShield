How it works

Four steps between you and a spoiler

You install it once. The shield does the rest, quietly, inside your browser.

  1. 01

    It reads the page

    As you scroll, it reads post text, titles, captions, hashtags and the name of the account, and looks for GTA 6 keywords in 6 languages.

  2. GTA 6 content, possible spoiler Posted by @Nuxhii
    02

    A black screen, right away

    The post is covered before you can read it. Videos inside it are paused and muted, so you can't hear a spoiler either.

  3. 5 Showing in 5… Cancel
    03

    5 seconds before you see

    Curious? “Show anyway” starts a countdown you can cancel at any time. It also stops by itself if you scroll away or switch tabs.

  4. @Nuxhii Allowed
    04

    Trust who you trust

    Allow an account right from the black screen, or from the popup. If the account can't be identified for sure, the post stays hidden.

Features

Built to cover the whole web

One extension. No account, no subscription, no catch.

Your feeds, and the rest of the web

Dedicated protection for X, YouTube (Shorts too), Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Google (AI overviews and ads too). Threads and every other site are covered by a general fallback.

2 blocking modes

Extreme hides anything about the game. Intermediate only hides posts that pair GTA 6 with a risky word or risky content.

Keywords in 6 languages

English, French, Spanish, Italian, German and Portuguese: the game, its characters, its places, its hashtags and spoiler words.

Your own keywords

Add any word or name you never want to see. Your keywords work in both modes.

Safe reveal

Spoilers stay blocked. If you still want to see one, a 5-second countdown runs first, so a misclick never reveals anything. You can cancel it at any time, and set it from 0 to 30 seconds.

Silent videos

Hidden videos, Shorts, Reels and TikToks are paused and muted until you choose to show them.

Account allowlist

Let trusted accounts through with a profile link or an @handle. Search, pause, and import or export your list in JSON.

Four themes, live counters

Sunset, in the style of this site, and its dark version, plus light Vice City and Dark. Counters show what was hidden on the page and in total.

100% local

Detection runs on your device. No server, no account, no analytics, and not a single network request in the code.

Install

Ready in about a minute

No store and nothing to pay: the extension comes straight from GitHub. It works in Chrome and every Chromium-based browser (Edge, Brave, Opera…).

Download the ZIP
  1. 1

    Download

    Use the button above, or on GitHub click Code, then Download ZIP.

  2. 2

    Unzip and keep the folder

    Put it somewhere it can stay, such as Documents. The browser reads the extension from it every time it starts.

  3. 3

    Open the extensions page

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar (Edge: edge://extensions).

  4. 4

    Turn on Developer mode

    It's the switch at the top right of the page.

  5. 5

    Load unpacked

    In the unzipped folder, select the extension folder: the one that directly contains manifest.json.

  6. 6

    Pin it and reload your tabs

    Pin it from the puzzle icon, then press F5 on tabs that were already open.

At startup the browser may suggest disabling developer-mode extensions. That's normal for any extension installed this way: just close the message. Firefox and Safari are not supported.

FAQ

Questions, answered

It hid something that isn't a spoiler. Why?

Extreme mode is strict on purpose: any mention of the game is hidden. Switch to Intermediate in the popup to hide only real spoilers, or allow the account that posted it. If a keyword is too aggressive, tell us on GitHub, describing the post instead of pasting a spoiler.

How do I let an account through?

Two ways. On a black screen, click “Toujours autoriser ce compte” (always allow this account) and confirm. Or open the popup, go to the “Liste blanche” tab and paste the account's profile link or @handle. If the account can't be identified for sure, the content stays hidden, on purpose.

How do I add my own word?

In the popup, type it under “Mots-clés personnalisés” (custom keywords) and click “Ajouter”. It works in both modes.

How do I turn it off for a moment?

Use the switch at the top of the popup, and turn it back on whenever you're ready. You can also pause only the allowlist with the switch in its tab.

Nothing is blocked on a page I just opened. Why?

Reload the tab (F5). After an install or an update, the browser doesn't add the extension to tabs that were already open.

Does it collect or send my data?

No. Detection runs locally and the code makes no network request at all. The browser will warn that the extension can read the sites you visit: that's how it finds spoilers on every site, and what it reads never leaves your device.

Why isn't it on the Chrome Web Store?

It's distributed for free, straight from GitHub, so anyone can check that what they install is exactly the public code. Installing it takes about a minute: see the steps above.
